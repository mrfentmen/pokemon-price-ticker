**Tagline:** Watch Pokémon card prices like a stock ticker

**Category:** Shopping

**Full description**

Pokémon cards are basically stocks now — so track them like one. PokéTicker turns any card search into a live price ticker right from your toolbar.

Search any card — Charizard, Pikachu, Umbreon, whatever you're holding — and add it to your watchlist. Each card shows its real TCGplayer market price for the variant that matters (holofoil, normal, or reverse), with a trend arrow computed from Cardmarket's 1d/7d/30d sale averages so you can see momentum at a glance. A scrolling ticker tape across the top of the popup keeps your whole collection moving in real time, and it auto-refreshes while the popup is open.

Priced cards rank first in search, so brand-new promos without market data yet never bury the cards you can actually trade. Click any card to jump straight to its TCGplayer listing.

It all lives in a small popup — no new tab, no clutter. If the price feed drops, your last quotes stay on screen with how old they are, color-coded from green (fresh) to red (long offline). Your watchlist is stored locally, and nothing is tracked.

Free to use forever. If the ticker pays for itself, a coffee is always appreciated. Found something broken? Send a quick note to contactae2000@gmail.com.
