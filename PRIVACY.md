# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

A hosted copy of this policy is available at https://mrfentmen.github.io/privacy-policies/pokemon-price-ticker.html

PokéTicker is a free extension. It is built to be private by default.

## What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The extension:

- Stores your watchlist (card names, ids, and the last prices you saw) locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. This data never leaves your device.
- Makes network requests to the public Pokemon TCG API (api.pokemontcg.io) to search cards and fetch current market prices. The requests carry no personal data and no account information. A descriptive User-Agent string identifies the extension, which is standard etiquette for public APIs.

## What we do not collect

- No personal information, names, emails, or addresses
- No health, financial, or payment information
- No authentication credentials
- No communications
- No location data
- No web history or browsing activity
- No clicks, keystrokes, or activity tracking

## Sharing

We do not sell or transfer any user data to third parties. We do not use user data for any purpose other than running the extension's single purpose of showing Pokémon card prices.
